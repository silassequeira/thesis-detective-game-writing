## 8.2.3 Doors as state made visible (~1 page)

- **Flag-driven doors:** a door opens when its flag is set, animating over 0.5s. If the flag was already set (for example after loading a save), it starts open.
- **Gates by evidence:** locked doors open through world actions that need specific evidence, not by walking up to them.
- **Outline as signal:** the gold proximity outline marks interactables. A door's outline is switched off for good once it opens, so the outline tells the player whether the door is still locked.
- **Link back to 8.1:** this is the flag architecture seen from inside the world.

## 8.2.4 Seeing only where you are: room-level visibility (~1.5 pages)

- **Mechanism:** `GameStateManager` shows one room root at a time. `RoomTrigger` volumes in the doorframes switch rooms as the player walks through. Everything else is hidden.
- **Co-visible rooms:** some doorways keep a neighbour visible. The Interrogation Room shows the Observation Room, the Bathroom keeps the corridor, and the Locked Room keeps the Evidence Room.
- **"FOW" layers:** each room has a fog-of-war group that is suppressed when it should not hide the view, and restored when the player leaves.
- **Why:** it limits what the player can see to what the character can know, and it keeps rendering cheap. Say which of the two you intended, and don't claim both without confirming.
- **Dream return:** the scene reload suppresses the initial transition so the player returns to the room they left.

## 8.2.5 Camera and framing (~1.5 pages)

- **Placement:** the camera follows the player at an offset of (-20, 20, 0) with a lazy follow (dead zone 2 × 1.5, smoothing 0.25s).
- **Zoom:** scroll zoom runs from 2.5 to 5 (the scene sets the zoomed-out size to 5, not the code default of 9). It is disabled while a panel is open or the player is locked.
- **Framing for the interrogation:** during dialogue the camera drifts 15% toward the speaker and zooms to 65% of the default size (about 3.25). It never zooms out for an interaction, and it restores the player's earlier zoom afterwards. The locked-room fall sequence uses the same framing.
- **Movement:** input is remapped to camera space, so up on the keyboard always means away from the camera.
- **Figure:** the same room at default zoom and at interaction framing.

## 8.2.6 Light, shadow and atmosphere (~1.5 pages)

- **Lighting:** per-room light groups (the Evidence Room has 3, the Corridor 4, the Observation Room 3, the Interrogation Room 2, the Bathroom 2, the Locked Room 3) and light probe groups. The GeometryAttempt dump lists no directional light, so the interior is lit by local lights. The Ending scene has one, disabled.
- **Baking tool:** an editor script creates a "low-spec moody" bake with GPU lightmapper settings, 64/128/64 samples, a 1024 maximum lightmap size, and OpenImage denoising. Present this as a decision made under hardware limits, and confirm it is the asset your scenes use.
- **Blob shadows:** a raycast places a quad under each character and scales and fades it with height. Say plainly that this replaces real-time character shadows.
- **Dust:** a particle effect in the Locked Room (220.9 normal, 710.5 disturbed). A "disturb" call exists, but I found nothing in the scripts that calls it, so don't claim it reacts to the player unless you wired it in the scene.
- **Darker wall sets:** the dump has `WallsDark` (9 objects) and the Ending scene has `DarkenedWalls`. Say what they are for once you confirm it.

## 8.2.7 The other scenes (~1.5 pages)

## 8.2.8 Space and time (~0.5 page)

- **Travel costs the clock:** the clock drains in real time at 1:1 (`Time.deltaTime / 3600`), so walking between rooms costs game time, though a small amount. The big time costs are the skip objects (the chair and the printer) and delayed evidence.
- **Link:** tie this to the kairos argument in 8.1.5 and the HUD in 8.3.6.
