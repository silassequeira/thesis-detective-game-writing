The player will control detective Julian Hammond Stoker or detective "Stoke", where he will have to convict one of the two suspects for murder. 

Who is Julian Stoker ? 
Stoker started as a country detective who stood out in solving a murder case related to a killing spree that happened in the are where he grew up in, he's method of interrogation is based on Inquiries and Confrontation to get people to slip up information that helps the case. Stoker is still relatively new as a detective but showed potential which lead him a position of Homicide Detective in the Police Precinct: Oakhaven Central – 4th District Station, moving to the big city. Since he's been assigned his first case, he needs to prove his worth to the captain in order to show that he can handle and own up to his role as a detective, but failing this initial case will get him fired from that position and back to the country side. He as a mold and dust allergy so he always carries with him an  Adrenaline Vial (AV) and an Auto Injector (AI), just in case he enters a place that triggers a bad allergic reaction that puts his life at risk.

### Setting and Physical Space

The game will take place inside the The Police Precinct: Oakhaven Central – 4th District Station. Located in a neighborhood called Gallow’s Creek, within the fictional city of Oakhaven.  

The explorable location will consist of 1) Interrogation Room - where one of the two suspects is waiting to be questioned. 2) Observation Room - Captain Miller (Chief Police of the station) and Officer "Buck" Buckley.  3) Evidence Room - where most of the reports can be obtained by accessing the police computer and also contains a locked door that leads to a case storage room with old archived police cases. 4) Corridor Room - That allows the player to enter all these different rooms 

The characters will consist of 1) Captain Miller (Chief Police of the station), 2) Officer "Buck" Buckley (Addresses Captain's Requests, gets the suspects for questioning), 3) Elara Vance (Woman Suspect), 4) Kaelen "Kae" Ward (Male Suspect).
